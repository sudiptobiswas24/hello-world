O606: a plant's own tax number prints as a Tax ID, not as a GSTIN

A plant with no GST registration printed its company's other tax number
labelled "GSTIN" on every letterhead. printed_letterhead() labelled
whatever issuer() kept, and since O603 issuer() keeps the registration's
GSTIN where there is one, else the company's own tax ID. The settings
screen already calls that field "Other tax number".

- registered_tax_number() answers the number and what it is: "GSTIN"
  from the registration, "Tax ID" from the company.
- issuer() keeps the label beside the number (tax_label), so a reprint
  prints what was issued.
- A letterhead kept before this has no label. Every number it kept was
  printed as a GSTIN, so it reprints as one, as it was printed.
- registered_gstin() keeps its answer for the invoice foot that calls
  it.

Tests:
- tests_company_gstin: a plant with no registration prints "Tax ID
  DE123456789" and no "GSTIN", and the same values kept without a label
  print "GSTIN DE123456789".
- tests_rcm_cash: a newly issued self-invoice's kept letterhead now
  carries tax_label "GSTIN". The migration-built letterhead in
  KeptAsIssuedMigrationTests keeps none, as before.
- apps.gst passes (428 tests), as do the eight other modules that read
  letterheads or GSTIN lines (71).
