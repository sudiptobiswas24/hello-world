## The defect
The shelf replays each movement at its exact value (eight-place rates, an
average carried unrounded); the ledger books each document to the paisa.
So they part by paisas with nothing wrong in any one document:
- one kilo written off at an average of 10.005 moves the shelf 10.005 and
  the books 10.00;
- two doffs at 95.83505100 a kilo put 10,733.525712 on the shelf and
  10,733.52 in the books (apps/e2e/tests_plant_month.py,
  PlantFloorTests.test_the_shelf_moves_with_the_inventory_account, marked
  expectedFailure);
- giving production output its booked paisa alone moved the demo plant's
  ledger a paisa from its shelf, through a write-off elsewhere. Do not
  fix one path alone.

## Decided (do not reopen)
Each movement's money is a fact: the paisa its document booked (mistake
4). The replay sums those; cost_of_removing answers in paisa, and what a
path books is exactly what its movement records. The residues on
transfers, receipts and conversions exist only because a rate could not
say a total; once the movement carries its money they go.

## What done means
- For every path that writes a movement, in and out (receipts, issues,
  deliveries, returns both ways, transfers and clearances, adjustments
  and counts, production output and byproducts, re-batches, backflush,
  job work, consignment, landed cost and revaluations, voids of each), the
  shelf's value moves by exactly what the document's entry moved the
  inventory account. One test per path family, with figures that do not
  come out even (a 10.005 average; 95.83505100 a kilo), worked in a
  plain-Python script first.
- Every costing method: weighted average, FIFO, specific batch, standard,
  and the folds (`_keep_valuation_foldable`) read the same as a full
  replay.
- Rows written before the change: a data migration gives each its money
  as its document booked it where that can be read, and says what it
  could not. Test it with MigrationExecutor.
- audit_invariants gains a check that names any movement written without
  its money, so a new path cannot leave it out.
- The month's marker comes off; the demo plant's health check reads
  "Stock agrees with the ledger" with figures that do not come out even.
- Average costs are still read to eight places for pricing; only money
  is kept to the paisa.
