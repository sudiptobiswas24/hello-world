## 1. What does Odoo 19 Accounting and Invoicing offer (ledger, journals, close, bank, reconciliation, payments, currency, analytic and budgets, assets, deferrals, follow-ups, reporting, consolidation, digitisation, AI)?

### Takeaway
Community 19.0 is the **Invoicing** app (`account`): ledger, journals, taxes, fiscal positions, payment terms, credit and debit notes, payments, lock dates with exceptions, analytic plans, cash rounding, incoterms, accrual and automatic-entry wizards. Everything a finance team usually calls "Accounting" (bank reconciliation UI and rules, bank sync, batch payments, assets, deferrals, budgets, follow-ups, financial reports, tax-return workflow, loans, OCR, consolidation, AI) is Enterprise and is not in the supplied source, so for those I could read documentation only.

## 2. What does the Indian localisation (l10n_in) cover in 19?

### Takeaway
Community covers the chart, GST tax set (rates 1, 2, 5, 12, 18, 28, 40), place of supply, GST treatments, HSN/SAC, TDS and TCS sections with threshold alerts and a TDS wizard (including the new Income-tax Act 2025 sections 393 and 394), PAN/TAN/MSME fields, e-invoice (IRN), e-way bill, GSTIN status check and GSTR section tagging. GSTR-1 filing, GSTR-2B fetch and match, GSTR-3B and P&L (IN) reports, vendor e-invoice fetch and the HDFC-style vendor payment file are Enterprise (`l10n_in_reports`). I found no GSTR-9/9C and no ITC-04 in Odoo 19.

## 3. What is new or materially changed in 19.0 compared with 18.0?

### Takeaway
Accounting: the bank-matching UI and reconciliation models were reworked, tax returns and year-end became a checklist workflow (review, submit, pay), current-year earnings became an explicit appropriation entry, deferrals gained a "Deferred Date" and standardised "Months", the audit trail became an optional restrictive mode, and an annual report builder, AI app, ESG app and expense cards arrived. India: packaging was consolidated and renamed, GST filing became a validated, staged workflow, GSTR-3 became GSTR-3B (report only), TDS and TCS became separate toggles and the Income-tax Act 2025 section groups were added, and GST rates include 40 %.

## 5. Indian rules that apply to this plant (2026): what I verified and against what

### Takeaway
The plant's PP scrap sales attract TCS under section 394(1) of the Income-tax Act 2025 (successor of 206C(1)) at **2 % from 1 April 2026** (it was 1 %), with a buyer declaration route (Form 127) and a quarterly return (Form 143). The official statute and Income Tax Department pages could not be fetched (egress policy), so the 2 % rests on a government order reproduced by a news site, tax-site rate tables and a search snippet of the official section page.

### Cited Findings
- Scrap TCS: the Income Tax Department's section 394 page lists "Sale of scrap" at Sl. No. 4, seller collects; the search summary of that page reports 2 % — [incometaxindia.gov.in section 394](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-394-6) (page not fetchable here; snippet only). Rate raised from 1 % to 2 % for scrap, alcoholic liquor and minerals and cut from 5 % to 2 % for tendu leaves by the Finance Act 2026, from 1 Apr 2026 — [taxguru, TCS rates rationalised to uniform 2 %](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rates-rationalised-uniform-2-percent-1st-april-2026.html), [taxguru, TCS rates under section 394(1)](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rates-section-3941-w-e-f-01-04-2026.html). A Railway Board order dated 17 Jun 2026 applies "@ 2 % as per section 394(1) of Income-Tax Act, 2025 (as amended by Finance Act, 2026)" to scrap sales and introduces Form 127 in place of Form 27C — [staffnews.in](https://www.staffnews.in/2026/06/tax-collection-at-source-tcs-on-scrap-sale.html).
- Conflicting figures: a search summary of other pages (terra-insight, tdsman) says 1 % under 394(1) Sl. 4 — [terra-insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/tcs-scrap-sale-section-394-auto-component-india/), [tdsman](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/05/tcs-on-sale-of-specified-goods-section-3941-206c1/); I weigh the Railway Board order and the multiple rate tables above them, but the enacted text was not read. One site also ties a "2 %" claim to section 206CE (buyer without PAN) — [disytax](https://disytax.com/section-206ce-scrap/) — so some 2 % mentions may be that higher rate.
- No threshold applies to scrap: one chart lists "No limit" — [taxguru rate chart](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rate-chart-tax-year-2026-27-income-tax-act-2025.html).
- What counts as scrap (old section 206C Explanation, case-law commentary): waste and scrap from manufacture or mechanical working of materials that is not usable as such; Gujarat High Court read the manufacture qualifier onto scrap; the ITAT and Rajkot special bench disagree about traders; Madras High Court held cotton waste reused as raw material is not scrap — [casemine commentary](https://www.casemine.com/commentary/in/interpretation-of-'scrap'-under-section-206c-of-the-income-tax-act:-a-comprehensive-analysis/view), [taxguru, every scrap sale is not liable](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/every-scrap-sale-is-not-liable-to-tax-collection-at-source-tcs.html).
- Buyer declaration: Form 127 (successor of 27C) under section 394(2) and Rule 212 of the Income-tax Rules 2026, for goods bought for manufacturing, processing, production or power generation rather than trading; optional; PAN mandatory or the seller collects; seller uploads details to the e-filing portal by the 7th of the following month — [Form 127 FAQs](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-127-faqs), [Rule 212](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-212-1).
- Return forms from Q1 of tax year 2026-27: TCS 27EQ becomes Form 143; 26Q becomes Form 140; 27Q Form 144 (Business Today says 27Q is 140; most sources say 144); the TCS return follows the TDS calendar (31 Jul for Q1) — [tdsman mapping](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/03/new-tds-tcs-forms-it-act-2025-mapping-with-old-forms/), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/tax/story/income-tax-rules-2026-govt-renumbers-key-forms-tax-audit-now-form-26-pan-tds-itr-forms-changed-519996-2026-03-11).
- Sale of goods TCS (206C(1H)): omitted from 1 Apr 2025 — [Tally help](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tcs-changes-in-budget-2020-tally/), [ebizfiling](https://ebizfiling.com/blog/section-206c-of-income-tax-act/). Odoo 19's legacy TCS group still lists "206C(1H): Sale of Goods"; its 2025-Act group has no such line ([account_tax_report_tcs_data.xml](file:///tmp/claude-0/-home-user-hello-world/2ec41226-46f2-5ea2-a139-e69651007cc2/scratchpad/odoo/src/addons/l10n_in/data/account_tax_report_tcs_data.xml)).
- TDS under the 2025 Act (secondary rate charts): section 393(1) table; contractor payments 1 % (individual/HUF) or 2 % (others), thresholds ₹30,000 single and ₹1,00,000 aggregate; professional 10 %, technical 2 % (₹50,000); rent 10 % land and building, 2 % machinery (₹50,000 a month); 194Q purchase of goods (Odoo's data labels it "exceeding ₹50 lakh") continues, but no result gave its rate or threshold — [studycafe chart](https://studycafe.in/tds-rate-chart-for-fy-2026-2027-under-income-tax-act-2025-420727.html), [gimbooks chart](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/tds-tcs-rate-chart-for-fy-2026-27/amp/).
- E-invoicing: taxpayers with annual turnover of ₹10 crore or more cannot report an e-invoice older than 30 days from 1 Apr 2025 (GSTN advisory of Nov 2024) — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/time-limit-of-30-days-for-reporting-e-invoice-on-irp-portal-for-taxpayers-with-aato-of-10-crores-and-above-gstn-update/), [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/time-limit-for-reporting-e-invoices-on-the-irp-portal). The ERP encodes 30 days as a warning only ([apps/gst/einvoice.py](file:///home/user/hello-world/apps/gst/einvoice.py)).
- GSTR-3B and IMS: the GSTN advisory of 7 Jun 2025 made auto-populated liability non-editable from July 2025 ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/gstr-3b-tax-liability-to-be-non-editable)); Table 3.2 was kept editable ([Taxo, 16 May 2025](https://taxo.online/latest-news/16-05-2025-gstn-issued-advisory-on-reporting-values-in-table-3-2-of-gstr-3b/)); the Invoice Management System lets the recipient accept, reject or hold an invoice, affecting GSTR-2B, and locking of auto-populated ITC "will be implemented later" — [ICMAI presentation](https://icmai.in/upload/Taxation/Courses/PPT/ACCGST14_PPT_2407_2025.pdf). The ERP's 2B match knows no IMS action.
- E-way bill: GSTN advisory of 29 Jul 2026 put the proposed enhancements (Ship-To GSTIN mandatory, voluntary closure), planned for 1 Aug 2026, on hold — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/e-way-bill-changes-june-2026), [taxguru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gstn-hold-rollout-proposed-bill-enhancements.html).
- GST rates: rationalisation to 5 %, 18 % and 40 % (plus nil) from 22 Sep 2025 — [taxaj.com](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=843). **Rate for woven PP sacks (HSN 6305 33) and plastic scrap (HSN 3915) not established:** Credlix lists 6305 33 at 5 % ([credlix](https://www.credlix.com/hsn-code/63053300)), BUSY at 18 % ([busy.in](https://busy.in/hsn/hsn-63053300.md)); ClearTax lists 3915 at 5 % with 2020-era data ([cleartax](https://cleartax.in/s/waste-parings-scrap-of-plastics-gst-rates-hsn-code-3915)), RegisterKaro at 18 % ([registerkaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-3915)).
