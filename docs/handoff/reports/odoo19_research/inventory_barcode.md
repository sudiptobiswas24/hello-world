# Odoo 19 Inventory and Barcode apps compared with this ERP's Stores (inventory) module

Researched 2026-10-14. Reference is Odoo 19.0. Scope: Inventory app, Barcode app, and the Purchase/Manufacturing pages that Inventory depends on. Valuation and landed cost are covered because Inventory owns them.

**How to read the tables.**
- **19 vs 18** is NEW, CHANGED or same. I derived it from a page-level diff of the `odoo/documentation` repo, branch `origin/18.0` against `19.0`. No Odoo 18 source was available, so a docs change can mean the page was written late rather than the feature being new. Where that matters I say so.
- **Ed.** is CE (Community) or EE (Enterprise). The 19.0 docs never label an edition. I used the community source tree (`addons/*/__manifest__.py`, LGPL-3). A module present there is CE. A module absent from it is marked EE*, an inference. The tree is a manifests-only checkout (no models or views), so code-level claims are limited to the `data` file lists in the manifests.
- **ERP** is have, partial or missing. The evidence is a link to the file I read.
- **Rel** is G (needed before go-live), S (soon after), L (later or not relevant), for a woven polypropylene sack plant in India. These are my judgements, each with a one-line reason. They are not sourced facts. They draw on `docs/PLANT_REVIEW.md` for how the plant works.
- Link text: `doc` is the Odoo 19 docs page, `18` is the Odoo 18 page, `src` is the Odoo 19 source manifest, `code`, `test` and `ui` are files in this ERP. The docs site was unreachable from the sandbox, so I read the rst from the local clone of https://github.com/odoo/documentation (19.0, commit 2472f7d). The odoo.com URLs are the published equivalents of those paths. Source manifests come from https://github.com/odoo/odoo/tree/19.0 (commit 042b6f9).
- Web evidence: odoo.com and third-party sites could not be fetched (proxy 403). Only search-engine summaries of third-party blogs were available. They are weak evidence and used only to corroborate, never as the sole basis.

## Q1. What is new or materially changed in Odoo 19.0 compared with 18.0 (Inventory and Barcode)?

### Takeaway
The substantive 19 changes are: (1) a rebuilt valuation and accounting model (valuation recorded on stock moves, perpetual entries posted at invoice plus one closing entry, periodic Anglo-Saxon now supported, a manual "Adjust Valuation"); (2) packages (pack-in-pack, richer package types, packagings merged into units of measure); (3) replenishment (a Just-in-Time page, Horizon days replacing Visibility days, MTO becoming a setting); (4) spreadsheet inventory dashboards; (5) Barcode count and EPC changes. Most other 18 to 19 doc changes are UI relabelling (Details link, Count Inventory, Confirm) and not new features.

### Cited Findings
- **Valuation rebuilt.** The new page states "This documentation is for Odoo 19 or later" and carries a "Changes in Odoo 19" table. The row values:
  - Periodic Continental: manual closing in 18, automated closing in 19.
  - Periodic Anglo-Saxon: not supported in 18, fully supported in 19.
  - Perpetual entries: invoices plus every move in 18, invoices plus one closing entry in 19.
  - Invoices to issue, prepaid expenses, bills to receive and deferred revenues: not supported in 18, supported in 19.
  - Accounting valuation: needs inventory in 18, accounting only in 19.
  - It also claims faster performance and fewer journal entries.
  Source: [doc cheat sheet](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/inventory_valuation/cheat_sheet.html).
- **Why it changed.** Before 19, Perpetual posted real-time entries at each stock movement, which hurt performance, general-ledger clarity and auditability. From 19 it impacts the stock valuation account at invoice level, and a closing entry handles gaps. Source: [doc cheat sheet](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/inventory_valuation/cheat_sheet.html).
- **Valuation is recorded on stock moves.** Each move records Value, Remaining Quantity and Remaining Value. A product's Total Value is the sum of Remaining Value over inbound moves that still have remaining quantity. An "Adjust Valuation" action on the Moves Analysis report corrects one move's value with a description. Source: [doc operations valuation](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/inventory_valuation/operations_valuation.html).
- **Stock Valuation Layers dropped from the docs.** The strings "Stock Valuation Layer" and "SVL" appear in two 18.0 inventory/barcode pages and in none in 19.0. The lower-case phrase "valuation layer" survives in 19.0 only in `valuation_by_lots.rst` (a heading and a few sentences about a lot's latest layer), so the word remains in use even though the model is no longer named. The 19.0 `stock_account` manifest lists no `stock_valuation_layer_*` view files, but does list `views/product_value_views.xml`, `report/stock_avco_audit_report_views.xml` and `report/stock_valuation_report.xml`. Sources: [src stock_account](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/stock_account/__manifest__.py). A third-party migration-guide title ("valuation layers to direct values") and a vendor description point the same way, but one consultancy page still cites the layer model, so treat the removal as likely and not confirmed ([ERP Heritage](https://erp.erpheritage.com.au/blog/odoo-1/odoo-19-stock-valuation-layers-direct-values-migration-guide-1)).
- **Valuation docs reorganised.** The 18.0 pages `product_management/inventory_valuation/using_inventory_valuation` and `inventory_valuation_config` were deleted. New pages are `inventory_valuation/cheat_sheet`, `operations_valuation` and `scrapped_inventory_valuation`; `landed_costs` and `valuation_by_lots` were moved. Source: git name-status diff of [documentation repo 18.0..19.0](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/compare/18.0...19.0).
- **Pack-in-pack is a new page.** Packages can be nested via "Put in Pack" with a package type. The package form gains Location and Container fields. A Packages smart button filters on "Main Packages", and a Remove button un-nests. The operation type gets a "Set Package Type" option. Source: [doc pack-in-pack](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/configure/pack_in_pack.html). The 19.0 `stock` manifest lists `views/stock_package_views.xml`, `stock_package_history_views.xml`, `stock_reference_views.xml` and `wizard/stock_package_destination_views.xml`, consistent with a reworked package model ([src stock](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/stock/__manifest__.py)). A third-party summary also lists "packs nested inside packs" as new ([Cybrosys, search summary only](https://www.cybrosys.com/odoo/odoo-19-features/)).
- **Package types restructured.** The form has Configuration, Dimensions and Capacity tabs. Capacity is by storage category and needs Multi-Step Routes. "Package Use" (Reusable or Disposable box) sits on the package type, and "Move Entire Packages" moved to the operation type's Barcode App tab. Source: [doc package](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/configure/package.html).
- **Packagings merged with units of measure.** The setting is now "Units of Measure & Packagings". The menu is "Units & Packagings". A sales packaging is set on the product's Sales tab ("Upsell & Cross-Sell") and a purchase packaging on the vendor line's "Unit". The 18.0 section "Routes for packaging" (a packaging-specific route) was removed, as was the packaging-barcode section on the barcode setup page. Sources: [doc packaging](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/configure/packaging.html), [18 packaging](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/configure/packaging.html), [doc uom](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/configure/uom.html).
- **Replenishment.** A new Just-in-Time page defines the forecasted date (current date plus the lead times of the replenishment process) and "To Order" (total demand on the forecasted date). "Horizon days" replaces 18's "Visibility days": the 18 reordering-rules page has a Visibility days section, 19 has none, and the 19 setting is a company-level "Replenishment Horizon" under Advanced Scheduling plus a sidebar field on the manual report. Sources: [doc JIT](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/just_in_time.html), [doc reordering rules](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/reordering_rules.html), [18 reordering rules](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/reordering_rules.html).
- **MTO is a setting.** 19 enables "Replenish on Order (MTO)" under Settings, Logistics. The 18 page told users to unarchive the archived MTO route after enabling Multi-Step Routes. Sources: [doc MTO](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/mto.html), [18 MTO](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/mto.html).
- **Suggest quantities (Purchase app) is a new page.** In the RFQ's product catalog a "Suggest" toggle proposes order quantities from forecasted demand or the last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 12 months, or the same month or quarter last year, with a percentage adjustment. Source: [doc suggest](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/purchase/advanced/suggest.html).
- **Cross-dock page removed.** 18.0 had `daily_operations/cross_dock`. In 19.0 the page is gone, the "Cross-Dock" route bullet was removed from `use_routes`, and the word "cross-dock" no longer appears in the inventory docs. Whether the route survives in code is unknown. Sources: [18 cross dock](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/shipping_receiving/daily_operations/cross_dock.html), [doc use routes](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/shipping_receiving/daily_operations/use_routes.html).
- **Dashboards.** `reporting/dashboards` is new (Warehouse Daily Operations, Operation analysis, Warehouse Metrics). 18's `reporting/aging` ("Stock valuation dashboard") was deleted. Source: [doc dashboards](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/reporting/dashboards.html).
- **Scrap.** In 19 the default scrap location is "Inventory adjustment" (18 used a dedicated Virtual Locations/Scrap location). A scrap order has an optional Scrap Reason. A location of type Inventory Loss can carry a Loss Account so scrap reaches the Profit and Loss report. The location-form checkboxes "Is a Scrap Location?" and "Is a Dock Location" are gone from the 19 docs. Sources: [doc scrap](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/scrap_inventory.html), [doc scrap valuation](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/inventory_valuation/scrapped_inventory_valuation.html), [doc locations](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/use_locations.html).
- **Barcode changes.**
  - "Count Entire Locations" moved from a setting to a per-count action on the Count Inventory screen.
  - "Show Quantity to Count" (shown by default in 18) became a per-request "Show Expected Quantity" checkbox on "Request a Count", with expected quantity hidden by default.
  - The button was renamed Count Inventory.
  - EPCs can now be generated in Odoo: export the "Electronic Product Code" field from a Moves list, needing a GTIN-14 barcode on the product. The 18 page said the GS1 Encoder/Decoder tool must be used.
  - Barcode Lookup now needs an API key for everyone. The 18 page said Odoo Online needed no configuration.
  Sources: [doc barcode adjustments](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/operations/adjustments.html), [doc EPCs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/operations/retrieve_epcs.html), [doc lookup](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/setup/barcodelookup.html).
- **Dispatch management.** The 19 page adds the Mapbox integration as a configuration step for delivery routes, and requires weight and volume on products so vehicle capacity can be evaluated. Source: [doc dispatch](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/shipping_receiving/setup_configuration/dispatch.html).
- **Unverified third-party claims about 19.** Cybrosys's summary lists "replenish-on-order as a setting", "custom lot/serial numbers on the product form" and "a deadline for the replenishment rule". I could confirm the first only. I could not find the other two in the docs. The Ecosire articles describing a supplier-reliability reordering engine and a "Resupply" route cite no release notes, and I discarded them. Sources: [Cybrosys](https://www.cybrosys.com/odoo/odoo-19-features/), [Ecosire](https://ecosire.com/blog/odoo-19-new-features).

### Inferences
- The ERP was designed around "derived, not stored" ledger replay and immutable posted documents (`CLAUDE.md`). Odoo 19's move-level value, remaining quantity and "Adjust Valuation" is a different philosophy. The ERP should not copy "Adjust Valuation"; its reversal-and-variance route is the stricter design.
- Of the NEW items, only pack-in-pack, Horizon days and EPC generation are genuinely new capabilities. The others are reorganisation. None is a go-live need for this plant (see Q10).
- Packagings becoming units of measure aligns with how the ERP already models a bale of 500 sacks as an `ItemUnit` conversion (Q3).

### Gaps
- No Odoo 18 source tree was available, so "new in 19" is a docs-diff judgement. Official 19.0 release notes were unreachable.
- Cross-dock: could not tell whether the route still exists in 19 code.
- I did not read every changed page line by line. Skimmed or skipped: carrier connector pages beyond Shiprocket, and the Manufacturing pages changed in 19 (e.g. `work_in_progress.rst` is new) other than scrap, by-products and dismantle. Those belong to the manufacturing researcher.

## Q2. Warehouses, locations, putaway, storage categories and routes (multi-step receipt/delivery, MTO, dropship, cross-dock, resupply, consignment)

### Takeaway
Odoo CE covers all of this. The ERP has multi-step receiving, transit warehouses, consignment (vendor-owned and customer-owned), drop-ship and a bin hierarchy, but no multi-step delivery, no putaway-rule table, no storage-category capacity and no cross-dock. For a one-site-plus-depots sack plant, none of the missing items is needed before go-live.

## Q3. Lots, serials, expiry, packages, packaging and units of measure

### Takeaway
The ERP matches Odoo on lot and serial tracking, FEFO, traceability and lot-level costing, and is ahead on quality-hold gating. It is behind on generic packages (it has bales only), pack-in-pack (new in 19), lot label printing and expiry sub-dates. It has no barcode field on items.

## Q10. Community vs Enterprise split, and the go-live priority view for a woven PP sack plant

### Takeaway
Nearly everything in Odoo 19 Inventory is Community; the Barcode app, carrier connectors, Quality, Shop Floor and MPS are Enterprise (inferred from absence in the community tree). Measured against this plant, I found no Odoo 19 Inventory or Barcode feature that is missing from the ERP and also needed before go-live. The soon-after list is item and location barcodes with scan-driven counts and receiving, cycle counts, weight-basis landed cost, putaway rules and a stock KPI dashboard.
