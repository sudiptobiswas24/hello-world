# Odoo 19 HR apps and Indian payroll compared with this ERP's People module

## 1. What does Odoo 19 offer in each HR app (Employees, Payroll, Time Off, Attendances, Expenses, Appraisals, Recruitment, Referrals, Skills, Fleet), which of it is new in 19, Community or Enterprise, and what does this ERP have?

### Takeaway
Odoo 19 is broad on employee records, self-service, work entries, overtime rules, accrual plans, expenses, appraisals and recruitment. Payroll, India payroll, Appraisals, Referrals and the Salary Configurator are Enterprise only, and the rest is Community. This ERP is deeper than Odoo on payroll posting integrity, piece-rate pay, PF/ESI/PT arithmetic, statutory dues tracking and guards between leave, attendance and posted runs. It is shallow or missing on employee master data, payslip output and employee self-service, bank payment files, salary advances, overtime rules, leave policy depth, expense policy, appraisals and recruitment.

**1G. What this ERP has that Odoo's documentation does not describe (verified in code)**

- Contract labour registers (licence, headcount ceiling, workers) ([contract_labour.py](/home/user/hello-world/apps/hr/contract_labour.py)), gratuity owed set against the provision ([gratuity.py](/home/user/hello-world/apps/hr/gratuity.py)), PF ECR and ESI files from a posted run ([statutory_files.py](/home/user/hello-world/apps/hr/statutory_files.py)).

## 2. What does Odoo's Indian payroll cover (PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS under section 192, Form 16, 24Q, bonus, gratuity, LWF, leave encashment), and what do Indian law in 2026 and the plant need on top?

### Takeaway
Odoo 19's India localisation (Enterprise) documents PF with a 15,000 cap option, professional tax, ESIC, LWF, gratuity as a monthly employer accrual, contract benefits, six statutory or payroll reports and India time-off rules (sandwich and optional holidays). Its documentation names TDS only in passing and describes no tax regime handling, Form 16, 24Q, statutory bonus, leave encashment or full and final settlement. This ERP already does PF, ESI, PT arithmetic, ECR and ESI files, bonus and gratuity accruals and dues tracking, but three 2026 legal changes hit it directly: the EPF wage ceiling (reported at 25,000 from 17 September 2026), the statutory bonus calculation ceiling (higher of 7,000 and the minimum wage), and the salary TDS regime of the Income-tax Act 2025.

**2B. Indian law as of 14 October 2026 (searches only; official pages were not retrievable, see Gaps)**
- Labour Codes: the four codes (Wages 2019, Industrial Relations 2020, Social Security 2020, OSH 2020) came into force on 21 November 2025, repealing 29 laws, except provisions repealing the EPF and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 ([Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/papers-publications/thought-paper/cracking-the-code-the-new-labour-laws-at-a-glance-1/), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-benefits-compensation/1730372/cracking-the-code-the-new-labour-laws-at-a-glance), [SCC Online on the Ministry FAQs, 26 April 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/04/26/ministry-faqs-four-labour-codes-india-2026/)).
- Central rules: the Code on Wages (Central) Rules 2026 were notified on 8 May 2026 and repealed 17 sets of rules including the Payment of Bonus Rules 1975 ([Keka](https://www.keka.com/compliance/acts/payment-of-bonus-act-1965)); the Social Security central rule became effective on 8 May 2026 but the ESI provisions are not fully operationalised ([Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://www.lkslaw.com/insights/articles/status-of-esi-compliance-under-the-code-on-social-security-2020-an-analytical-review)).
- Wages definition: wages are basic, dearness allowance and retaining allowance, with excluded components capped so that wages are not less than 50 per cent of total remuneration, the excess added back ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/retirement/pension/gratuity-under-new-labour-codes-rules-eligibility-and-impact-explained), [Lexplosion on the Ministry FAQs](https://lexplosion.in/the-ministry-of-labour-and-employment-issues-additional-faqs-on-all-four-labour-codes/)). Sources differ on whether the base is total remuneration or cost to company.
- Gratuity: fixed-term employees become eligible after one year of service, pro rata, applicable from 21 November 2025 ([Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/print/v2/content/47799/indias-new-labor-codes-expand-gratuity-payment-rules.pdf), [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/the-ministry-of-labour-and-employment-issues-additional-faqs-on-all-four-labour-codes/)).
- Full and final settlement: wages payable within two working days of removal, dismissal, retrenchment or resignation, for all employees with no wage ceiling; leave encashment and bonus fall inside, gratuity and PF have their own timelines ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/two-working-days-code-wages-employee-final-settlement-552780), [PocketHRMS](https://www.pockethrms.com/blog/2-day-full-and-final-settlement-under-new-labour-codes-2025/)).
- Statutory bonus: the Payment of Bonus Act was repealed from 21 November 2025 and Chapter IV of the Code on Wages (sections 26 to 41) governs; minimum bonus is 8.33 per cent of wages or Rs 100, whichever is higher, and payable within eight months of the accounting year-end ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/bonus-under-code-on-wages-2019-allocable-surplus-set-on-set-off/), [LawSikho](https://lawsikho.com/blog/?p=6979)). Two notifications of 25 August 2026 (S.O. 4711(E)) fix eligibility at wages up to Rs 21,000 a month and calculate bonus "as if wages were Rs 7,000 or the minimum wage fixed by the Central Government, whichever is higher", deemed in force from 21 November 2025, for establishments where the Central Government is the appropriate government ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/26/bonus-rules-under-code-on-wages-eligibility-calculation/), [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/59059/mole-determines-21000-wage-ceiling-for-bonus-eligibility-under-code-on-wages-2019/), [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/?p=152253)). A factory is normally in the State Government's sphere; the state's own notification was not found.
- EPF wage ceiling: a notification of 29 May 2026 (S.O. 2702(E)) kept Rs 15,000 ([Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/central-government-retains-the-wage-ceiling-of-rs-15000-per-month-for-the-purpose-of-epf-contribution/), [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-153.html), [Bombay Chamber](https://bombaychamber.com/legal-news/central-government-notifies-rs-15000-as-the-pf-wage-ceiling-under-code-on-social-security/)). Later reports say the Cabinet approved on 16 September 2026 and S.O. 5109(E) of 17 September 2026 raised it to Rs 25,000 for EPF, EPS and EDLI, superseding S.O. 2702(E), effective from the date of publication, with a mid-month transition for September payroll ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/epfo-wage-ceiling-hiked-to-25-000-labour-ministry-issues-gazette-notification/article-200466/), [ComplianceAge](https://updates.complianceage.com/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-effective-17-september-2026/), [Nitso Tech](https://www.nitsotech.com/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-payroll-guide/), [LexComply](https://lexcomply.com/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-2026-applicability-impact-6aacb4e6437a6)). The later notification is reported by several secondary sources; none is the gazette.
- ESIC: the mandatory coverage ceiling stays Rs 21,000 a month (Rs 25,000 for persons with disability) per an ESIC letter of 4 June 2026; the new wage definition may widen coverage ([Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://www.lkslaw.com/insights/articles/status-of-esi-compliance-under-the-code-on-social-security-2020-an-analytical-review), [AscentHR](https://ascent-hr.com/notification/esic-clarification-regarding-the-implementation-of-code-on-social-security-2020/)).
- Salary TDS: section 392 of the Income-tax Act 2025 replaces section 192 from 1 April 2026; Form 138 (earlier 24Q) is the quarterly salary return under Rule 219 of the Income-tax Rules 2026; Form 130 replaces Form 16, due 15 June after the year; the employee declaration (earlier 12BB) is reported as Form 124 by one source ([Income Tax Department Form 138 page](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/fn-138), [TaxGuru on Form 130](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/form-130-tds-certificate-salary-replacing-form-16.html), [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/form-130-tds-certificate-salary-income-tax-act-2025-45463.html/), [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/tds-and-tcs-changes-from-april-2026)). Sources disagree on one name (Form 143 versus 138 for the return); the Department's page supports 138.
- New regime is the default; a standard deduction of Rs 75,000 and a section 87A rebate making income up to Rs 12 lakh tax-free (Rs 12.75 lakh of salary) ([Keka](https://www.keka.com/tds-on-salary), [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/tax/govt-clarifies-rs-75000-standard-deduction-for-salaried-employees-in-new-tax-regime)). Section numbers of the rebate under the new Act are inconsistent across sources.
- Contract labour: the OSH Code replaced the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act 1970 from 21 November 2025, applies at 50 or more contract workers (20 before, though some states had 50), bars contract labour in core activities with exceptions, and has a single registration for establishments with ten or more workers ([Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/contract-labour-under-the-new-regime-an-overview), [Singhania](https://singhania.in/blog/contract-labour-reforms-under-the-osh-code)).
- Professional tax nationally: Article 276 caps it at Rs 2,500 a person a year ([CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/professional-tax-india-state-wise-guide-fy-2026-27/)).

## 4. What does West Bengal add (labour welfare fund, professional tax, minimum wages, state rules and factory obligations), and how does this ERP stand?

### Takeaway
The plant is registered in West Bengal. The West Bengal Labour Welfare Fund is Rs 3 from the employee and Rs 30 from the employer per half year, paid on 30 June and 31 December with returns by 15 July and 15 January, according to a Deloitte alert and an Indian Tea Association circular that quote the Labour Department's 19 November 2024 notification. West Bengal professional tax moved to a new salary schedule from 1 October 2026 (nil to Rs 20,000, then Rs 100, 140, 170 and 208), according to several consultancy reports of a 16 September 2026 notification. I could not read any official West Bengal source: every official host returned a proxy 403. All figures below are therefore secondary and must be confirmed against the gazette and the portals before use. This ERP can hold both as configured components but has no state concept, no dated slabs, no LWF type, no registration numbers and no return files.

### Cited Findings

**4A. West Bengal Labour Welfare Fund (Labour Welfare Fund Act 1974)**
- Rates: employer Rs 30 and employee Rs 3 per half year, per a Labour Department notification dated 19 November 2024 published in the Kolkata Gazette (Extraordinary) on 2 December 2024, applying from 1 January 2024; the employer share was Rs 15 before ([Indian Tea Association circular](https://www.indiatea.org/uploads/circular/1739174532.pdf), [Deloitte tax alert, December 2024](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/in/Documents/tax/in-deloitte-tax-alert-enhancement-of-labour-welfare-fund-employers-contribution-in-west-bengal-noexp.pdf)). The official notification PDFs were surfaced by search but not opened: [Labr-576641 dated 19-11-2024](https://www.labour.wb.gov.in/sites/default/files/upload/notice/Labr-576641%20Dated-19-11-2024.pdf) and [Notification Labour Welfare Board](https://www.labour.wb.gov.in/sites/default/files/upload/notice/Notification-Labour-Welfare-Board.pdf).
- Conflict: several compliance sites still list Rs 15 for the employer ([HREngage 2026 page](https://hrengage.ai/compliance/lwf/west-bengal), [FactoHR](https://factohr.com/labour-welfare-fund/west-bengal/)); Rs 30 appears in the notification-based sources and in [Simpliance](https://simpliance.in/India/LEI/labour_welfare_fund/state-wise-details/west-bengal).
- Frequency and dates: half-yearly, contributions on 30 June and 31 December, returns to the Board by 15 July and 15 January ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/west-bengal-labour-welfare-fund), [Simpliance](https://simpliance.in/India/LEI/labour_welfare_fund/state-wise-details/west-bengal)). A 2020 notification requires digital payment of both shares (Simpliance).
- Coverage: establishments with ten or more employees; managerial and supervisory staff earning more than Rs 1,600 a month are excluded ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/west-bengal-labour-welfare-fund)). The West Bengal Labour Welfare Fund (Amendment) Act 2023 changes sections 2 and 9, and its text shows the exclusion wage ceiling changing from Rs 1,600 to Rs 10,000; its commencement needs a state notification and I found none ([India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19875/1/the_west_bengal_labour_welfare_fund_%28amendment%29_act%2c_2023.pdf)).

**4B. West Bengal professional tax (State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act 1979)**
- Old salary slabs, in force to September 2026: nil up to Rs 10,000; Rs 110 for 10,001 to 15,000; Rs 130 for 15,001 to 25,000; Rs 150 for 25,001 to 40,000; Rs 200 above 40,000 ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/professional-tax-west-bengal), [DCPS](https://www.dcpsol.com/west-bengal-professional-tax-slabs-revised-2026-notification-1607-ft)).
- New salary slabs from 1 October 2026: nil up to Rs 20,000; Rs 100 up to 30,000; Rs 140 up to 50,000; Rs 170 up to 1,00,000; Rs 208 above 1,00,000. Reported as Notification No. 1607-F.T. dated 16 September 2026 in the Kolkata Gazette (Extraordinary), final after a draft 1407-F.T. of 18 August 2026 drew no objections; rates for professionals and businesses start 1 April 2027 ([SGCMS](https://www.sgcms.com/regulatory-updates/west-bengal-overhauls-professional-tax-new-schedule-notified/), [DCPS](https://www.dcpsol.com/west-bengal-professional-tax-slabs-revised-2026-notification-1607-ft), [United Consultancy](https://unitedconsultancy.com/revision-of-west-bengal-professional-tax-rates-effective-from-01st-october-2026/), [AscentHR](https://ascent-hr.com/notification/revised-professional-tax-schedule-west-bengal/), [GDSN draft note, 24 August 2026](https://www.gdsnco.in/2026/08/24/west-bengal-proposes-revised-professional-tax-slabs-effective-1-october-2026/)). Sources differ on the notification number and on whether the Rs 100 band begins above 20,000 and ends at 25,000 or 30,000.
- Monthly deduction by the employer, with a Certificate of Enrolment (the person's own liability, within 90 days of liability) and a Certificate of Registration (as deductor); portals named are wbprofessiontax.gov.in and the WBComTax portal ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/professional-tax-west-bengal), search summary).
- Returns: the annual employer return is Form III; its due date has been extended in many years (for example 15 June 2024 electronic and 30 June 2024 paper) so the base date is not established ([GreytHR extension notice](https://greythr.com/notifications/extension-of-due-date-for-filing-professional-tax-returns-in-west-bengal-revised)). I found no reliable source for the monthly payment due date; penalty figures conflict between sources.

**4C. Minimum wages and other state obligations touching payroll**
- West Bengal revises minimum wages for scheduled employments twice a year; a notification (No. 30/Stat/14/RW/24/2023/LCS/JLC) revised rates effective 1 July 2026, and variable dearness allowance was updated by circulars of 22 June 2026 ([Simpliance](https://simpliance.in/India/LEI/govt_notification/west-bengal/the-west-bengal-minimum-wages-notification-jul-2026-9751), [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/57235/government-of-west-bengal-revisied-minimum-wages-for-various-scheduled-employments-effective-from-july-1-2026/)).
- For the "Plastic Industry" schedule a third-party aggregator lists, for 1 July to 31 December 2026, monthly Zone A rates of Rs 10,558 (unskilled), 11,615 (semi-skilled), 12,777 (skilled) and Zone B rates of Rs 9,760, 10,733, 11,807; Zone A is municipal corporations, municipalities, notified areas and townships ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/en-in/ai/work-in-india/minimum-wage/22516-west-bengal/22854-plastic-industry/)). Which schedule covers woven polypropylene sack manufacture was not found; it must be confirmed with the Labour Commissionerate.
- State rules under the labour codes: West Bengal published draft Code on Wages Rules by Notification No. Labr/103/Law of 13 July 2026 (45-day objection period), and a draft OSH rules notice; a 8 July 2026 report says the new state government is working towards implementing the codes ([DCPS](https://www.dcpsol.com/draft-west-bengal-code-on-wages-rules-2026), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/west-bengal-government-set-to-implement-labour-codes-541845-2026-07-08), [Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://employmentlaw.lkslaw.com/states/west-bengal/wages)). Final state rules were not found, so the statutory bonus rules, wage periods and deduction limits for a West Bengal factory are in transition.
- Factory records: the West Bengal Factories Rules 1958 and Payment of Wages Rules 1958 are published ([LegitQuest](https://www.legitquest.com/act/west-bengal-factories-rules-1958/CE18)); form numbers for the register of adult workers and the notice of work periods conflict between two guides ([Deskera](https://www.deskera.com/blog/west-bengal-form-13-register-of-adult-workers)), so I give none. A claim that wages are due by the 7th of the next month comes from one HR-software page about contract labour and is unverified ([Keka](https://www.keka.com/compliance/acts/west-bengal-contract-labour-regulation-and-abolition-rules-1972-complete-hr-guide)). This ERP already warns when last month's wages are unposted after the 7th ([WAGES_BY](/home/user/hello-world/apps/web/checks.py)).

### Inferences
- West Bengal professional tax after 1 October 2026 is a small tax at low wages (nil to Rs 20,000, at most Rs 208), so errors are small in rupees but certain to be questioned if the old slab (Rs 110 to Rs 200) is used. Rate G: configure with an effective date from the first run.
- If the minimum wage governs the bonus ceiling, the West Bengal Plastic Industry figures above (Rs 9,760 to 12,777 a month, from a third-party aggregator and for a schedule not yet confirmed as the plant's) exceed Rs 7,000 by 39 to 83 per cent. Bonus at 8.33 per cent on a flat 7,000 is Rs 583 a month against Rs 813 to Rs 1,064, so a flat cap would understate it by about 28 to 45 per cent (my arithmetic). Rate G to set the ceiling per employee class and keep it dated; the plant's accountant should confirm the schedule, zone and whether the state or central notification governs.
- LWF is the smallest amount but a fixed date: the next deduction is the December 2026 run. Rate G for a configured component and S for the return.
- W5 supports G: a minimum-wage table that warns when a worker's pay or daily wage is below the schedule would protect the plant, and it feeds the bonus ceiling.
