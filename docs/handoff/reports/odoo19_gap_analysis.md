# Indian statute, not Odoo parity, gates go-live

In eight of the ten modules (all but Accounts and People) the research found no Odoo 19 feature that the plant needs on its first day and the ERP lacks, and the ERP goes beyond Odoo in finite scheduling, quality statistics, job work, Indian TDS and MSME rules, and ledger integrity. The go-live gap is instead **sixteen build-or-configure items, seven in Accounts and nine in People, and almost all of them are Indian statute or payroll operations rather than Odoo parity**. At the head of the list is **TCS on the plant's scrap sales, which the ERP does not have at all** (reported at 2% under section 394(1) of the Income-tax Act 2025 from 1 April 2026, though the enacted text was not read). Behind it come a printed tax invoice with no buyer GSTIN, HSN or place of supply, an e-invoice cancellation that is not built, and payroll basics: a bank payment file, printed payslips, an advance ledger, dated professional-tax slabs (West Bengal changed its schedule on 1 October 2026), a half-yearly Labour Welfare Fund component needed before the December run, and EPF and bonus ceilings that 2026 notifications may have moved. What is new in Odoo 19 mostly misses this list: the headline changes are a stock-valuation redesign the ERP should not copy, an AI layer, a JSON-2 API and HR contracts folded into employee records, while Odoo's own GST filing and India payroll are Enterprise-only and silent on Form 16, quarterly salary returns, statutory bonus and final settlement. The ERP trails Odoo on reach (no portal, WhatsApp, inbound mail, two-factor login, API tokens or pivot reports) and on HR master data, which the research rates "soon after". The g20 gate, not yet pushed, closes 50 defect rows (21 of the 44 the notes cite) and leaves 70 open, so go-live work narrows to the statutory list plus a few open correctness rows. Every legal figure rests on secondary sources because the official sites were blocked, and the confirmation table in the last section lists 20 items for the plant's accountant or tax adviser.

## Odoo 19's headline changes barely touch a sack plant, and its India depth sits behind Enterprise

The comparison reads Odoo 19's documentation, the Community source manifests (638 modules, commit 042b6f9) and a documentation diff between branches 18.0 and 19.0 ([Odoo 19.0 addons](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/tree/19.0/addons), [18.0 to 19.0 documentation diff](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/compare/18.0...19.0)). Odoo's pages almost never state an edition, so "Community" here means the module sits in that tree and "Enterprise" is inferred from its absence; only WhatsApp is stated to be Enterprise-only ([Odoo WhatsApp docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/productivity/whatsapp.html)). No Odoo 18 source was available, so "new in 19" is a documentation-diff judgement, and the docs sometimes lag the code (the 19 BoM page still describes a worksheet tab whose fields the Community code has removed) ([mrp_routing.py, 19.0](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/mrp/models/mrp_routing.py)). Odoo's release notes were unreachable. Third-party blogs claim a hash-chained audit snapshot, "AI bank reconciliation" and a rewritten MRP scheduler; none appears in Odoo's own 19 documentation, so none is used here ([Ecosire on accounting](https://ecosire.com/blog/odoo-19-accounting-new-features-2026), [Ecosire on manufacturing](https://ecosire.com/blog/odoo-19-manufacturing-mps-quality-mrp)). Ratings are G (needed before go-live), S (soon after) and L (later or not relevant). They are the researchers' judgement for a woven polypropylene sack plant in West Bengal, no plant staff were consulted, and "have" means the code exists, not that a clerk has used it: the register's first entry is that no real person has yet used the ERP ([docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md)).

Much of the HR and Time Off "newness" is reorganised documentation, not changed behaviour, and the largest page-count changes (payroll localisations for other countries, Point of Sale) are irrelevant to a factory ([18.0 to 19.0 documentation diff](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/compare/18.0...19.0)).

## The ERP leads where the plant earns its money and trails on output and reach

The picture by module is consistent: where a sack plant makes or loses money (planning, costing, quality, job work, Indian tax rules) the ERP is at least level with Odoo 19 and often ahead; where it loses is printed output, communication, analysis tooling and master-data breadth. The summary below names each module's Odoo counterpart, the verdict, and the largest holes, and the paragraphs after it give the evidence. The office application registers 27 Production screens, 24 "How it's made" screens, 14 Plant screens and 14 Quality screens ([registry.ts](../frontend/src/app/registry.ts)).

|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | Sales and CRM (Community; commissions, subscriptions, rental, WhatsApp, AI Enterprise) | Level or ahead on quote-to-cash; CRM thin (1 of 25 rows present) | Quote templates, section lines, lead merge, inbound mail, WhatsApp (S); portal, e-signature (L) |
| Purchasing | Purchase (Community; OCR Enterprise) | Ahead on approvals, budgets, inspection, landed cost | RFQ PDF and mail, receipt reminders, Group RFQ rule, Suggest (S) |

### Sales and Purchasing cover every must-have, but the CRM is a notebook

Across 98 Sales, CRM and Purchase feature rows the ERP is present on 26, partial on 34 and missing on 38, but the 30 rows rated G contain no missing feature: 21 are present and 9 partial. Four of the nine partial rows (price lists, unit-aware order lines, vendor prices and bill matching) are held back by defects inside features that exist: price-list currency and unit (O78, O79) and vendor-price unit (O98) are fixed in g20, while the purchase price check that ignores an agreed discount (O91) and the never-called `match_report` (O109) are still open. The other five are differences of shape: no global discount wizard, invoicing policy set per order rather than per product, no ship-complete field, a request for quotation that cannot be mailed as a PDF although the purchase order can, and bill policy per order ([apps/sales/models.py](../apps/sales/models.py), [apps/purchasing/models.py](../apps/purchasing/models.py)). Six missing rows are rated S: quotation templates, section and note lines, lead merge, email-to-lead, SMS and WhatsApp, and vendor receipt reminders. The CRM is the thinnest area because it is deliberately "the rep's own notebook": fixed stages, no mail or WhatsApp reading, a rule-of-thumb lead score ([docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md), known limit 34). Odoo's equivalents are Community except WhatsApp, which needs an Enterprise licence and a Meta WhatsApp Business Platform account ([Odoo WhatsApp docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/productivity/whatsapp.html)), so the ERP could add its own WhatsApp send without adopting Odoo's app.

The new Purchase features of 19, Suggest and Group RFQ, map onto parts the ERP already holds (reorder rules, requisitions, seasonal forecasts), which makes them cheap wins rather than foundations ([Group RFQ source](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/purchase_stock/models/stock_rule.py)). Open rows that bear on trading go-live are the credit limit adding currencies at face value (600 USD plus 380 EUR reads 980 against 1,000 when the true figure is 1,018.00; O77), a hand-typed price passing the approval rule whenever any agreed price exists (O99), and an approval withdrawn on re-price that re-asks only the budget (O167). The commission defects (O74 to O76) are fixed in g20. Portals, online payment and e-signature are rated L: buyers confirm by purchase order, mail or WhatsApp and pay by bank transfer, and Odoo's own bill OCR does not read Indian taxes ([invoice digitisation docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting/vendor_bills/invoice_digitization.html)).

### Stores lacks only the scanning convenience layer

Nearly all of Odoo 19 Inventory is Community, while the Barcode app, carrier connectors, Quality, Shop Floor and MPS are not in the Community tree, and no Odoo 19 Inventory or Barcode feature is both missing from the ERP and needed before go-live. The ERP has multi-step receipt with a quality hold, lot tracking and a recall walk from granule lot to bale, units of measure with sack-weight conversion, counts and adjustments whose reasons carry the account, scrap and a tested scrap-sale flow, returns booked at `cost_of_removing()`, and four costing methods per item and warehouse with an as-of-date valuation reconciled to the ledger ([apps/inventory/costing.py](../apps/inventory/costing.py), [apps/inventory/reports.py](../apps/inventory/reports.py)). Its valuation design differs from Odoo 19 on purpose, so the new invoice-time perpetual entry and "Adjust Valuation" stay L. What is missing is the scanning layer: the ERP scans a purchase-order number at the gate, bales onto a delivery and roll labels from production, but `Item` has no barcode field and `StorageBin` none either ([apps/inventory/models.py](../apps/inventory/models.py), [apps/inventory/bins.py](../apps/inventory/bins.py), [apps/manufacturing/barcode.py](../apps/manufacturing/barcode.py)); there are no assigned or cycle counts, no putaway rule table, no weight-based landed-cost split, and no fill-rate or on-time dashboard.

Defects here are the larger risk than missing features. The write-down that booked an explicit cost (O3), the importer reading 8-place opening costs into a 4-place field (O4) and deletable posted counts (O205) are fixed in g20. Still open are a cancelled put-away leaving its receipt move (O11), a reservation reading its shelf before the lock (O39), a receipt that never asks a lot's release status so a quality-rejected lot can reach a pickable shelf (O123), and a pick that ignores tape sitting on creels (O133). A new g20 row, O210, records that landed freight on goods cleared out of inspection and since gone is classed as cost of sales whatever they went into. If the plant keeps depots in other states, the ERP has no e-way bill payload for an inter-branch transfer, which Odoo's `l10n_in_ewaybill_stock` provides ([manifest](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in_ewaybill_stock/__manifest__.py)). Plastic-waste extended producer responsibility reporting is in neither product and was not researched.

### Production, How it's made, Plant and Quality lead except on operator instructions

Odoo 19 Community carries the manufacturing core (BoMs, by-products, operations, work centres, work orders, order costing, subcontracting, unbuild, scrap, repairs, a basic maintenance app), and Odoo's Community settings screen shows MPS, Quality and Barcode as Enterprise upsells ([mrp settings view](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/mrp/views/res_config_settings_views.xml)). Every row rated G in this group is a row the ERP already has: four-level BoM trees, by-products and waste, routings with machine rates, finite scheduling, operation costing with variances posted at close, perpetual work in progress, shift handling, OEE, planned and breakdown maintenance, inspection release and certificates, and job work. The holes, all rated S, are operator step instructions on a tablet (the largest functional gap against Odoo's Shop Floor, since the job card prints limits but not instructions), photo and worksheet quality checks, engineering-change stages and multi-approver rules (G if customers audit recipes), vendor, serial and warranty fields on a machine, operator-raised maintenance requests, and automatic machine counters so that short stops reach OEE. Freight on goods issued to production now goes to production cost under the owner's decision 20 (O48, fixed in g20).

Still open and relevant here: a lot that passed its own inspection stays released when a batch it was made from is rejected later (O207), and a mandatory-plan failure at the machines can still be released on the spot by a supervisor's concession (O204). The owner decided both on 14 October (decision 23): mandatory failures wait for the Quality Manager as advisory ones already do under decision 22, and a later rejection holds what is still in stock of the lots made from it; both are scheduled for g21. Odoo 19 has no recycling feature at all; its manufacturing and repairs documentation does not mention recycling polypropylene ([documentation tree](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/tree/19.0/content/applications/inventory_and_mrp)), so granulating waste would be one BoM, a granulator work centre and an inspection plan in either product, and the ERP already treats regrind as a first-class loop ([apps/manufacturing/bom.py](../apps/manufacturing/bom.py)). The project's plant review of 5 October is out of date on several points (maintenance and quality screens exist, scrap sale works), so it should not be used as the gap list ([docs/PLANT_REVIEW.md](../docs/PLANT_REVIEW.md)).

### Accounts is deeper on Indian rules and thinner on Odoo's Enterprise layer

Community Odoo 19 is the Invoicing app: ledger, journals, taxes, fiscal positions, payment terms, credit and debit notes, payments, lock dates with exceptions and analytic plans. Everything a finance team usually calls Accounting (bank reconciliation rules, bank sync, batch payments, assets, deferrals, budgets, follow-ups, financial reports, tax-return workflow, loans, OCR, consolidation) is Enterprise, so comparing with Community alone would understate Odoo ([Odoo accounting docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting.html)). Against that, the ERP is deeper on place of supply, TDS threshold modes with catch-up, customer TDS receivable, the 45-day MSME schedule with an acceptance rule, ITC-04 for job work, GSTR-2B matching from the portal's file, advances in GSTR-1 and a ledger where posted documents are corrected only by reversal ([apps/accounting/tds.py](../apps/accounting/tds.py), [apps/purchasing/msme.py](../apps/purchasing/msme.py), [apps/gst/itc04.py](../apps/gst/itc04.py)). It is thinner on bank-matching rules, XLSX statement import, payment files, cheque printing, unrealised exchange differences, written-down-value and revaluation of assets, deferrals, a cash flow statement, a day book, cash rounding, consolidation and typed journals, almost all rated S or L.

The go-live blockers sit elsewhere and are all Indian. The next section lists them: TCS on scrap, tax-invoice content, IRN cancellation, TDS data, a complete edit log, and the unseeded chart of accounts and GST taxes. Several open rows also touch Accounts: a period can close while its depreciation is uncharged (O29), the fixed-asset register is not footed against the ledger in the daily checks (O34), and `Account.currency` is read by nothing (O24). The money rows O49, O51 and O52 (last-part rounding, past statements reading today's facts) are fixed in g20, and a new row O209 records that re-pointing a middle allocation may leave a paisa on the control account.

### People has sound payroll integrity but thin master data and no payslip output

Odoo's Payroll, India payroll, Appraisals, Referrals and Salary Configurator are Enterprise (absent from the tree), while Employees, Time Off, Attendances, Expenses, Recruitment, Skills and Fleet are Community ([Odoo 19.0 addons](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/tree/19.0/addons)). The ERP's payroll is deeper than Odoo's documentation describes: piece-rate pay by metres woven and day-rated pay by attendance, a rate change inside a period paying each rate for its own days, statutory dues tracked per liability and month with remittances tied to real bank payments, guards between leave, attendance and posted runs, gratuity set against the provision, contract-labour registers, and PF ECR and ESI files built from a posted run ([apps/hr/payroll.py](../apps/hr/payroll.py), [apps/hr/statutory_files.py](../apps/hr/statutory_files.py), [apps/hr/gratuity.py](../apps/hr/gratuity.py)). It is shallow on employee master data (an employee adds only `uan` and `esi_number` to the party record, which can hold bank accounts with IFSC but has no date of birth, gender, dependents or nominee, and nothing checks that a paid worker has an account), has no contract entity, no payslip in the print layer, no bank payment file (each slip is settled by its own payment), no advance or loan ledger, one-level leave policy with no accrual milestones or compensatory leave, overtime typed as hours times a per-person rate with no approval step, and no off-cycle runs ([apps/hr/models.py](../apps/hr/models.py), [apps/core/documents.py](../apps/core/documents.py)).

Odoo is not the benchmark for the payroll gaps that matter. Its India payroll documents PF with a 15,000 cap option, professional tax read from the contract and the company's state, ESIC, a fixed Labour Welfare Fund contribution, gratuity as a monthly accrual and six reports; it mentions TDS once and describes no regime handling, Form 16, quarterly return, statutory bonus, leave encashment or full and final settlement ([India payroll docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/hr/payroll/payroll_localizations/india.html)). The leaver recovery that the notes found unbuilt (O28) is fixed in g20, with a new row O215 for settling the receivable it creates; new rows O213 and O214 record that PF and ESI wages must never be reduced by a recovery and that the half-wages deduction cap is applied only on a final slip. Payroll items beyond the nine G rows include off-cycle runs, a salary register, work-entry types, rotating weekly offs, an all-staff leave-balance report and a check that contract-labour registers, modelled on the repealed 1970 Act's forms, still suit the OSH Code (all S).

### Settings is strong on control and weak on reach

The platform comparison is lopsided in a useful way. The ERP has twenty roles (the register still says 17), applies model permissions to reads as well as writes, gives actions their own permissions, makes role-giving a two-person act when the giver lacks the role, refuses posted deletes, and rate-limits sign-in by name and by address ([apps/core/management/commands/setup_roles.py](../apps/core/management/commands/setup_roles.py), [apps/core/auth.py](../apps/core/auth.py)). It has no customer or vendor portal, no WhatsApp or inbound mail, no TOTP, passkey, SSO or session-timeout setting, no API keys (authentication is session or HTTP Basic only), no spreadsheet or pivot layer, no `.xlsx` writer and no user-built automation beyond a handful of cron commands ([config/settings.py](../config/settings.py), [apps/web/summary.py](../apps/web/summary.py)). Record history stores the names of fields changed rather than old and new values, and a change made through the Django admin or a script leaves no history line, which is why the edit log is a G item for Accounts. Custom fields are stored and shape-checked but nothing reads them: no filter, export, posting or paper ([apps/core/customfields.py](../apps/core/customfields.py)). Before go-live the platform items are already built (roles, history, attachments, SMTP, CSV import and export, GST payloads) with one operational catch: attachments live in the `media` volume, and the nightly `backup.sh` dumps only the database, so the runbook's manual copy of the volume must be scheduled or every record will say "attached" with nothing to open ([RUNBOOK.md](../RUNBOOK.md), [deploy/backup.sh](../deploy/backup.sh)).

### Where the ERP out-builds Odoo, and what that costs

The depth beyond Odoo is concentrated in the plant's own economics. Odoo's planner places one work order at a time into the first free slot of one work centre ([mrp_workcenter.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/mrp/models/mrp_workcenter.py)); the ERP schedules backward per machine with finite capacity, sequence-dependent changeovers and crew limits, and its master schedule checks rough-cut capacity before a run is committed ([apps/planning/capacity.py](../apps/planning/capacity.py), [apps/manufacturing/changeover.py](../apps/manufacturing/changeover.py), [apps/planning/mps.py](../apps/planning/mps.py)). Odoo 19's manufacturing, quality, maintenance and PLM documentation contains no mention of statistical process control, Cpk, AQL sampling or certificates of analysis ([documentation tree](https://github.com/odoo/documentation/tree/19.0/content/applications/inventory_and_mrp)), all of which the ERP implements, along with a calibration register that lists suspect inspections backwards and forwards ([apps/quality/spc.py](../apps/quality/spc.py), [apps/quality/sampling.py](../apps/quality/sampling.py), [apps/quality/calibration.py](../apps/quality/calibration.py)). ### GST: rates, e-invoice, e-way bill and filing

GST rates were rationalised to 5%, 18% and 40% (plus nil) from 22 September 2025 ([taxaj](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=843)), and Odoo 19's fiscal-position tax maps carry 1, 2, 5, 12, 18, 28 and 40 ([template_in.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/models/template_in.py)). The ERP seeds no taxes, so the accountant's list has to be loaded before the first invoice. The rate for woven polypropylene sacks (HSN 6305 33) is not settled: one listing gives 5% and another 18% ([Credlix](https://www.credlix.com/hsn-code/63053300), [BUSY](https://busy.in/hsn/hsn-63053300.md)); plastic scrap (HSN 3915) shows 5% in a 2020-era table and 18% on another site ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/waste-parings-scrap-of-plastics-gst-rates-hsn-code-3915), [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-3915)). Taxpayers with annual turnover of 10 crore or more cannot report an e-invoice older than 30 days from 1 April 2025 ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/time-limit-of-30-days-for-reporting-e-invoice-on-irp-portal-for-taxpayers-with-aato-of-10-crores-and-above-gstn-update/), [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/time-limit-for-reporting-e-invoices-on-the-irp-portal)); the ERP returns the age only as a warning because it does not know the plant's turnover. A GSTN advisory of 29 July 2026 put proposed e-way bill enhancements (Ship-To GSTIN mandatory, voluntary closure), planned for 1 August 2026, on hold ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/e-way-bill-changes-june-2026), [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gstn-hold-rollout-proposed-bill-enhancements.html)). A GSTN advisory made auto-populated GSTR-3B liability non-editable from July 2025 with Table 3.2 kept editable (whether the lock took effect was not verified), and the Invoice Management System lets a recipient accept, reject or hold an invoice, which changes GSTR-2B; the ERP's 2B match knows no IMS action ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/gstr-3b-tax-liability-to-be-non-editable), [Taxo](https://taxo.online/latest-news/16-05-2025-gstn-issued-advisory-on-reporting-values-in-table-3-2-of-gstr-3b/), [ICMAI](https://icmai.in/upload/Taxation/Courses/PPT/ACCGST14_PPT_2407_2025.pdf)).

Odoo 19 sends e-invoices and e-way bills through a GSP from Community modules, but GST filing, GSTR-2B fetch and match, vendor e-invoice fetch and the vendor payment file are Enterprise (`l10n_in_reports`), which Community settings prove with upgrade prompts ([res_config_settings_views.xml](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/views/res_config_settings_views.xml)). The ERP compiles GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, ITC-04 and a 2B match from an uploaded file and builds e-invoice and e-way bill payloads, but nothing is sent to the portal: a person uploads them, and a rejected payload is found out there ([docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md), known limit 8). Unbuilt in GSTR-3B are imports, 4(B) and 4(D) reversals and table 6. The g20 register still holds three open statutory rows on document content, two of them marked for the tax adviser to decide: the e-invoice ship-to block carrying the buyer's GSTIN when goods go to a third party (O152), the e-way bill's transaction type 2 for delivery to the buyer's other address (O153), and the "CHL" challan coding for a sales delivery (O166).

### TCS on scrap sales: the plant's one hard TCS case, absent from the ERP

The Income Tax Department's section 394 page lists "Sale of scrap" at serial 4 as a seller-collects item, and a search summary of that page reports 2% ([section 394](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-394-6)). Two TaxGuru notes report that the Finance Act 2026 raised the rate for scrap, alcoholic liquor and minerals from 1% to 2% from 1 April 2026 ([uniform 2%](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rates-rationalised-uniform-2-percent-1st-april-2026.html), [rates under 394(1)](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rates-section-3941-w-e-f-01-04-2026.html)), and a Railway Board order of 17 June 2026 applies "@ 2 % as per section 394(1)" to scrap sales and introduces Form 127 in place of Form 27C ([StaffNews](https://www.staffnews.in/2026/06/tax-collection-at-source-tcs-on-scrap-sale.html)). Search summaries of two other sites say 1% ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/tcs-scrap-sale-section-394-auto-component-india/), [TDSMan](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/05/tcs-on-sale-of-specified-goods-section-3941-206c1/)), and one ties a "2%" claim to the higher rate for a buyer without PAN under section 206CE ([Disytax](https://disytax.com/section-206ce-scrap/)); the research weighs the Railway Board order and the rate tables above those summaries but did not read the enacted text. One chart lists no threshold for scrap ([TaxGuru chart](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-rate-chart-tax-year-2026-27-income-tax-act-2025.html)). Section 206C(1H), TCS on sale of goods, was omitted from 1 April 2025 ([Tally](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tcs-changes-in-budget-2020-tally/), [eBizFiling](https://ebizfiling.com/blog/section-206c-of-income-tax-act/)), yet Odoo 19's legacy TCS group still lists it, while its 2025-Act group has "SI.4: Sale of scrap" and no sale-of-goods line ([legacy data](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/data/account_tax_report_tcs_data.xml), [2025 data](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/data/account_tax_report_tcs_it_act_25_data.xml)).

Whether the plant's polypropylene waste counts as "scrap" is a legal question, not a data one. The old definition covers waste and scrap from manufacture or mechanical working of materials that is not usable as such, the Gujarat High Court read the manufacture qualifier onto it, the ITAT and a Rajkot special bench disagree about traders, and the Madras High Court held cotton waste reused as raw material is not scrap ([Casemine](https://www.casemine.com/commentary/in/interpretation-of-'scrap'-under-section-206c-of-the-income-tax-act:-a-comprehensive-analysis/view), [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/every-scrap-sale-is-not-liable-to-tax-collection-at-source-tcs.html)). A buyer who purchases for manufacturing, processing, production or power generation may give Form 127 under section 394(2) and Rule 212; it is optional, the PAN is mandatory or the seller collects, and the seller uploads the details by the 7th of the following month ([Form 127 FAQs](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-127-faqs), [Rule 212](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-212-1)). Odoo 19 supplies the section, a tax per section and a "higher TCS" button that swaps in the highest-rate tax when the customer's PAN is missing ([account_invoice.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/models/account_invoice.py)), but the rates and thresholds live in a data file that was not in the source copy, so Odoo's own scrap rate is unknown; it generates no TCS return file, FVU or challan object in the Community India modules read, and the Enterprise part could not be inspected ([l10n_in_section_alert.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/models/l10n_in_section_alert.py)). A scrap invoice from this plant needs a TCS line, the buyer's PAN, the collect-or-declaration choice, a payable account, a monthly payment and a quarterly return listing; the ERP has none of them.

### Income-tax Act 2025 renumbering: names change, mechanics stay

The renumbering reaches every statutory deduction the plant touches. TCS under 206C(1) becomes section 394(1), salary TDS under 192 becomes section 392, and TDS on contracts, professionals, rent and purchase of goods moves into the 393 tables ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/tds-and-tcs-changes-from-april-2026)). Forms follow: 24Q becomes Form 138 under Rule 219 (the Department's own page supports this), 26Q becomes Form 140, TCS return 27EQ becomes Form 143, 27Q becomes Form 144 though one outlet says 140, Form 16 becomes Form 130 due 15 June, and the buyer's Form 27C becomes Form 127 ([Form 138 page](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/fn-138), [TDSMan mapping](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/03/new-tds-tcs-forms-it-act-2025-mapping-with-old-forms/), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/tax/story/income-tax-rules-2026-govt-renumbers-key-forms-tax-audit-now-form-26-pan-tds-itr-forms-changed-519996-2026-03-11), [TaxGuru on Form 130](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/form-130-tds-certificate-salary-replacing-form-16.html), [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/form-130-tds-certificate-salary-income-tax-act-2025-45463.html/)). Rate charts give contractor payments 1% (individual or HUF) or 2% with thresholds of 30,000 single and 1,00,000 aggregate, technical services 2% and professional 10% with a 50,000 threshold, and rent 10% on land and building or 2% on machinery with a 50,000 monthly threshold; none gave the purchase-of-goods rate or threshold, though Odoo's 2025-Act data labels that line "exceeding ₹50 lakh" ([StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/tds-rate-chart-for-fy-2026-2027-under-income-tax-act-2025-420727.html), [Gimbooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/tds-tcs-rate-chart-for-fy-2026-27/amp/), [TDS data](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/19.0/addons/l10n_in/data/account_tax_report_tds_it_act_25_data.xml)).

For the ERP the renumbering is mostly an entry job, because TDS sections, rates, thresholds and accounts are data kept on one screen; the screens and docs still say 26Q and challan 281, a renamed form and challan, and there is no TAN field, no return or FVU file, thresholds run per party rather than per PAN, and tax is not deducted on advances ([apps/accounting/tds.py](../apps/accounting/tds.py), [docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md)). Odoo 19 carries both generations of section groups side by side, activated by company toggles, a pattern the ERP could follow for a transition year. MSME dues sit in the same family of rules: section 43B(h) applies a 45-day limit to payments to micro and small suppliers (its number under the 2025 Act was not checked), Form MSME-1 is half-yearly (April to September by 31 October, October to March by 30 April), and interest runs at three times the bank rate compounded monthly ([SetIndiaBiz](https://www.setindiabiz.com/blog/45-day-msme-payment-rule), [Taxaj](https://www.taxaj.com/form-msme-1), [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/?p=5764)); the ERP counts the 45 days from the bill's date rather than acceptance of the goods, computes no interest and builds no MSME-1 ([docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md), known limit 17).

### Payroll under the Labour Codes: three ceilings and a closing-pay clock

The four labour codes came into force on 21 November 2025 and repealed 29 laws ([Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/papers-publications/thought-paper/cracking-the-code-the-new-labour-laws-at-a-glance-1/), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-benefits-compensation/1730372/cracking-the-code-the-new-labour-laws-at-a-glance), [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/04/26/ministry-faqs-four-labour-codes-india-2026/)). The Code on Wages (Central) Rules 2026 were notified on 8 May 2026 and repealed the Payment of Bonus Rules 1975 ([Keka](https://www.keka.com/compliance/acts/payment-of-bonus-act-1965)). Wages are basic, dearness allowance and retaining allowance, with excluded components capped so that wages are at least half of total remuneration, though sources differ on whether the base is total remuneration or cost to company ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/retirement/pension/gratuity-under-new-labour-codes-rules-eligibility-and-impact-explained), [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/the-ministry-of-labour-and-employment-issues-additional-faqs-on-all-four-labour-codes/)). Fixed-term employees become eligible for gratuity after one year, pro rata ([Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/print/v2/content/47799/indias-new-labor-codes-expand-gratuity-payment-rules.pdf)), and wages are payable within two working days of removal, dismissal, retrenchment or resignation, covering leave encashment and bonus, with no wage ceiling ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/two-working-days-code-wages-employee-final-settlement-552780), [PocketHRMS](https://www.pockethrms.com/blog/2-day-full-and-final-settlement-under-new-labour-codes-2025/)). The ERP's gratuity test is a hard-coded five years with no fixed-term one-year case, and it has no final-settlement statement or leave encashment, so the first leaver starts the two-day clock against a manual final run ([apps/hr/gratuity.py](../apps/hr/gratuity.py)).

The EPF ceiling is the most consequential unverified claim. A notification of 29 May 2026 (S.O. 2702(E)) kept 15,000 ([Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/central-government-retains-the-wage-ceiling-of-rs-15000-per-month-for-the-purpose-of-epf-contribution/), [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-153.html), [Bombay Chamber](https://bombaychamber.com/legal-news/central-government-notifies-rs-15000-as-the-pf-wage-ceiling-under-code-on-social-security/)); four later reports say the Cabinet approved a rise on 16 September 2026 and S.O. 5109(E) of 17 September raised the ceiling to 25,000 for EPF, EPS and EDLI, effective on publication with a mid-month transition for September ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/epfo-wage-ceiling-hiked-to-25-000-labour-ministry-issues-gazette-notification/article-200466/), [ComplianceAge](https://updates.complianceage.com/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-effective-17-september-2026/), [Nitso Tech](https://www.nitsotech.com/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-payroll-guide/), [LexComply](https://lexcomply.com/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-2026-applicability-impact-6aacb4e6437a6)). None is the gazette. If it stands, the ERP's EPS cap and any component ceiling of 15,000 are wrong from that month, and Odoo's own "cap Basic at 15,000" PF option would need the same change. The ESIC coverage ceiling stays 21,000 (25,000 for persons with disability) per an ESIC letter of 4 June 2026 ([Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://www.lkslaw.com/insights/articles/status-of-esi-compliance-under-the-code-on-social-security-2020-an-analytical-review), [AscentHR](https://ascent-hr.com/notification/esic-clarification-regarding-the-implementation-of-code-on-social-security-2020/)); the ERP keeps it as data.

Statutory bonus is now governed by Chapter IV of the Code on Wages, with a minimum of 8.33% of wages or 100 rupees and payment within eight months of the accounting year-end ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/bonus-under-code-on-wages-2019-allocable-surplus-set-on-set-off/), [LawSikho](https://lawsikho.com/blog/?p=6979)). Two notifications of 25 August 2026 (S.O. 4711(E)) fix eligibility at wages up to 21,000 and calculate bonus as if wages were 7,000 or the minimum wage, whichever is higher, deemed in force from 21 November 2025, but they are reported for establishments where the Central Government is the appropriate government, and a factory is normally a State matter whose notification was not found ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/26/bonus-rules-under-code-on-wages-eligibility-calculation/), [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/59059/mole-determines-21000-wage-ceiling-for-bonus-eligibility-under-code-on-wages-2019/)). The ERP accrues 8.33% of basic and DA capped at a flat 7,000 ([docs/RISKS.md](../docs/RISKS.md), known limit 19). The stakes are visible with an aggregator's West Bengal "Plastic Industry" minimum wages for July to December 2026, 9,760 to 12,777 a month, a schedule not confirmed as the plant's ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/en-in/ai/work-in-india/minimum-wage/22516-west-bengal/22854-plastic-industry/)): bonus at 8.33% on 7,000 is 583 rupees a month against 813 to 1,064 on those wages, so a flat cap would understate it by about 28 to 45%. The OSH Code replaced the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act from 21 November 2025, which may leave the ERP's registers, modelled on that Act's forms, out of date ([Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/contract-labour-under-the-new-regime-an-overview), [Singhania](https://singhania.in/blog/contract-labour-reforms-under-the-osh-code)).

### West Bengal levies: professional tax changed on 1 October 2026

Professional tax moved to a new salary schedule from 1 October 2026: nil up to 20,000, then 100, 140, 170 and 208 rupees a month in rising bands, reported as Notification 1607-F.T. of 16 September 2026, final after a draft of 18 August; the old schedule ran nil to 10,000, then 110, 130, 150 and 200 ([SGCMS](https://www.sgcms.com/regulatory-updates/west-bengal-overhauls-professional-tax-new-schedule-notified/), [DCPS](https://www.dcpsol.com/west-bengal-professional-tax-slabs-revised-2026-notification-1607-ft), [United Consultancy](https://unitedconsultancy.com/revision-of-west-bengal-professional-tax-rates-effective-from-01st-october-2026/), [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/professional-tax-west-bengal)). Sources differ on the notification number and on whether the 100-rupee band ends at 25,000 or 30,000. The Labour Welfare Fund is, per a Labour Department notification of 19 November 2024 applying from 1 January 2024, 30 rupees from the employer and 3 from the employee per half year, paid on 30 June and 31 December with returns by 15 July and 15 January, against an employer share of 15 earlier ([Indian Tea Association circular](https://www.indiatea.org/uploads/circular/1739174532.pdf), [Deloitte](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/in/Documents/tax/in-deloitte-tax-alert-enhancement-of-labour-welfare-fund-employers-contribution-in-west-bengal-noexp.pdf), [Simpliance](https://simpliance.in/India/LEI/labour_welfare_fund/state-wise-details/west-bengal)); several compliance sites still show 15 ([HREngage](https://hrengage.ai/compliance/lwf/west-bengal), [FactoHR](https://factohr.com/labour-welfare-fund/west-bengal/)). Minimum wages for scheduled employments were revised from 1 July 2026 ([Simpliance](https://simpliance.in/India/LEI/govt_notification/west-bengal/the-west-bengal-minimum-wages-notification-jul-2026-9751), [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/57235/government-of-west-bengal-revisied-minimum-wages-for-various-scheduled-employments-effective-from-july-1-2026/)), and the state published draft Code on Wages rules on 13 July 2026 ([DCPS](https://www.dcpsol.com/draft-west-bengal-code-on-wages-rules-2026)), so final state rules on wage periods and deductions were not found.

The ERP can hold both levies as configured components but has no state concept (the company is a singleton), no effective dates on slab rows, no seeded state sets, no LWF kind, no registration-number fields and no return files; it does track dues by month with an overdue flag and ties remittance to a bank payment ([apps/hr/payroll.py](../apps/hr/payroll.py), [apps/core/models.py](../apps/core/models.py)). Because a rate change on 1 October means editing bands, posted runs are safe (their lines are frozen), but recalculating an earlier month would misprice it unless slabs carry dates. The next LWF deduction date is 31 December 2026, so the component has to exist before the December run, which is why it belongs on the G list.

## Conclusion

The question "how does the ERP compare with Odoo 19" has a split answer that reframes the plan. On Odoo's own ground the ERP is level or ahead wherever a sack plant makes or loses money, and what it lacks there is mostly a convenience layer, the cheapest parts of which reuse models it already has. But the go-live gate is set by Indian law, where Odoo is no benchmark: its GST filing and India payroll are Enterprise, its TCS has an alert and a tax but no return, and its payroll documentation is silent on salary returns, bonus and final settlement. A roadmap copied from Odoo's feature list would add Suggest, Group RFQ and RFQ mailing, and would still leave scrap TCS, the payslip and the bank file unbuilt. What stays open after g20 is bounded: the sixteen items plus fifteen open correctness and statutory rows, much of it data entry with dates.

The second finding is that the past year makes "dated data, not code" the design rule for every statutory number. The GST rate change of September 2025, the Labour Codes, the Income-tax Act 2025 renumbering, a reported EPF ceiling change on 17 September, bonus notifications of 25 August and West Bengal's professional-tax schedule of 1 October all landed within about twelve months, and the ERP's hard-coded EPS cap and flat bonus ceiling are the places where it has not yet absorbed that. Two numbers, the EPF ceiling and the bonus ceiling, sit under every payroll item, and the 20-row confirmation table shows that neither has been read from a gazette. The first practical step is therefore not code: it is the accountant reading those gazettes, then the pilot, which is the only test that can show whether "have" means usable by the plant's clerks.
