O605: the demo plant's floor screens show its work

Eight floor screens showed nothing on the demo: Time booked, Bales,
Fabric rolls, Operator yield, Scrap by reason, the station morning
report, Step counts and Attendance. A person trying the plant's floor
on the demo could not see any of them work. The demo's own loom-exit
station could not weigh a roll either: the only run on the looms was of
a fabric with no specification.

The demo now does the floor's work through the floor's own functions:
record_roll, record_bags, book_scrap, pack, scrap.report and
TimeBooking.post.

- The 50 cm fabric (F-50) is woven on LOOM-05 from the inspected tape
  lot. Five rolls are weighed at the loom exit yesterday: one typed
  while the scale was being calibrated (approved by the supervisor), one
  declaring 1,060 m against 982.86 m from its weight.
- Two rolls are measured for GSM and cut into atta sacks on STITCH-1.
  The press counts 1,050 printed. The stitching tables count two bundles
  of 500, weighed against the bag specification, and book 18 spoiled
  for stitching and 12 for edge trim. The two bundles are pressed into
  one bale.
- The crews book their hours on four steps (performance 81-86%).
- The floor's four people are present for the last week, once late.
  No day is unpaid, so the posted and calculated pay runs read the same.
- FAB-50-87 and SACK-ATTA25 are kept by batch from the start, as a roll
  and a bundle each are one.

Seen on the screenshots and fixed here: the Rolls screen's Fabric
column read the specification's name, which is optional. Every roll of
F-50, which has a code and no name, showed no fabric. It now reads the
name, else the code.
