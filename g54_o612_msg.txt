O612: a consignment store takes only its own vendor's goods, is billed once, and a receipt is offered quarantine

O612 was registered as a screen saying the server refuses quarantine,
transit and consignment for a receipt while posting refuses only a
customer's material. Probing what posting does take found two defects
behind it, both in the books.

- Another vendor's goods could be received into a vendor's
  consignment store. They went in at no value, as that store's
  vendor's stock, and their order then billed 400 that was never
  accrued. Posting now refuses it, in the loop that serves receipts
  and returns alike, beside the customer's-material check.
- The consignment vendor's own delivery could be billed on its order,
  for goods the company did not own, and then billed again when drawn
  (draw_consignment raises its own order and bill). bill_limit() now
  leaves out what came on consignment (quantity_on_consignment).
  ordered_limit() keeps the old meaning for what an order is worth,
  which approvals quote. create_bill says the goods are on consignment
  rather than "already fully billed", and a typed bill is refused
  naming the quantity.

The screens, as registered:
- receivableWarehouse() is one rule for what a receipt may be put
  into: quarantine (goods made from batches quality holds must land
  there), and the order's own vendor's consignment store. Never
  transit, a customer's material or another vendor's store.
- The order page's Receive offers it; the Scan screen's offers it
  without consignment, the vendor being unknown there; the line adder
  offers it too.
- What Receive goes to without asking stays the company's own stock.
- WarehouseChoice's docstring now says what the server refuses.

Tests:
- WhatComesOnConsignmentIsBilledOnceTests: four tests, all failing on
  the old code. A refused receipt, an order that bills nothing, a typed
  bill refused naming the consignment, and a draw that still bills
  once.
- WarehouseChoice.test.ts covers the rule case by case.
- A browser test: Receive on the order page offers the quarantine bay,
  and the receipt lands in it. It fails on the old screens: no such
  option.
- The first version asked a query a row for what came on
  consignment, and test_the_lists_do_not_ask_once_per_row caught it on
  the purchase order list. The list's prefetch now brings each receipt
  line with its warehouse, and quantity_on_consignment reads that.
- The purchasing, accounting and inventory suites pass (2644), as do
  the purchasing, scanning and sales browser modules (37) and vitest
  (91). audit_invariants: no findings; makemigrations --check: none.
